Охуенный блять совет

A small Windows app for the legendary advice service fucking-great-advice.ru: shows advice as a text overlay on top of your desktop — from the tray, on startup or on a timer.

The app and its advice are in Russian.

DownloadFree · Windows 10/11Source code on GitHub

Features

  • Click the tray icon to get advice
  • Overlay on top of all windows, fades out on mouse hover
  • Advice on startup or on a timer
  • Offline icon when the service is unavailable
