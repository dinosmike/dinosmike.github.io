ReLight

Screen backlight for Adalight LED strips (Arduino) on Windows — the colors of the screen, music, cinema and rainbow effects on the wall behind your TV or monitor.

DownloadFree · Windows 10/11Source code on GitHub

Features

  • Screen — the strip repeats the colors along the edges of the screen
  • Cinema — skips black bars and gently breathes with the sound
  • Music and microphone — the strip reacts to sound and voice
  • One steady color with almost no load on the computer
  • 14 rainbow effects with an editor and a playlist
  • Its own mode for games and video players
  • Finds the strip's port by itself, LED count test and calibration
  • Tray menu and global hotkeys
  • Autostart, dark and light themes, English and Russian
